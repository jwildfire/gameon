ANGRYDUDES

Two armies, one giant slingshot. Launch your troops across the battlefield and flatten the computer's fort before you run out of dudes.

Launch screen mock-up. Your lineup waits by the slingshot on the left. The computer's fort sits on the right, built from crates, sandbags, brick and steel, with the General on top.

How a battle works

  1. 1

    Get your lineup

    Each battle gives you a set number of troops, like five. They wait in line next to the slingshot.

  2. 2

    Pull back, let go

    Drag back on the slingshot to aim and power up. The dotted trail from your last shot helps you adjust.

  3. 3

    Tap for the special

    Most troops have a mid-air move. Tap at the right moment for the biggest crash.

  4. 4

    Flatten the fort

    Knock out every enemy dude to win. Each troop you didn't need is worth 10,000 bonus points.
