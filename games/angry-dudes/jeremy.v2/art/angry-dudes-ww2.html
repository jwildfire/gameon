How a battle works

  1. 1

    Check your arsenal

    Each battle hands you a mix of troops, weapons, vehicles and planes. Each one has a set number of uses, like in Worms.

  2. 2

    Sling, fire, drive or call in

    Troops fly from the slingshot. Weapons aim from your bunker. Vehicles drive across. Planes come when you tap a spot.

  3. 3

    Tap for the special

    Almost everything has a trick: pop a parachute, fire the tank gun, drop the bombs. Timing is everything.

  4. 4

    Take the fort

    Knock out every Axis defender to win. Anything left in your arsenal earns bonus points.
