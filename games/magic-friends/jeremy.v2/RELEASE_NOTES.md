The first release of Magic Friends, Up We Go!

**Play it at https://jwildfire.github.io/magic-friends/**, or download `magic-friends.html` below and open it in any browser to play offline.

What's in v1.0:

- 60 levels across 12 worlds: four ocean worlds, two side-scrolling Fairy Island worlds, and six sky worlds.
- Three friends to play as: the mermaid swims up from the bottom of the sea, the fairy flutters through the island trees, and the unicorn carries everyone up through the clouds.
- A witch who causes trouble at the start of every level. She steals the mermaid's magic crown, locks the unicorn in a cage, and steals the sunbeams.
- Tap or click where you want to jump, or use the keyboard or the on-screen buttons.
- Easy, Normal and Harder modes. On Easy, falling floats you back to the last ledge you stood on.
- A victory parade after each world, story cartoons between the ocean, the island and the sky, and a big dance party at the sunrise.
- Progress saves in the browser.
