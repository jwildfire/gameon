The Very
Fancy Food Fight

Splat the fanciest party in town. Messiest wins!

Designed by Jeremy · A GameOn game

How to play
  • Throw: tap or click where you want the food to land. Hold and slide to aim, then let go.
  • Pick a food: pie, spaghetti, jelly or cake (keys 1 to 4). Cake breaks into chunks. Jelly bounces.
  • Duck! When a guest's hand goes up with a !, hold Duck (or Shift or Z) so their food flies over you.
  • Big splats: hit the chandelier three times, or the waiter's tray, for a huge mess.
  • Messiest wins: every splat you make scores. After each minute, whoever made the most mess gets the crown. Three rounds, most crowns wins the night.
  • Keyboard: arrows or WASD move the aim, Space throws, P pauses, M turns the sound on or off, H shows this help.
