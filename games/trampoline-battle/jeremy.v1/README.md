# Trampoline Battle

A two-player ragdoll brawl on a trampoline, designed with my kids. Take turns dropping fighters onto the trampoline. Everyone stays on and keeps fighting, and you score when you knock an enemy off.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any browser. It's one self-contained file and works on desktop, iPad and phones (turn the phone sideways).

## How to play

- **Drop:** drag to aim, then press DROP (or Space). A landing slams the trampoline and bounces everyone.
- **Fight:** ◀ ▶ or A/D to move, ↑ or W to jump, ↓ or S to slam, Space or F to punch.
- **Fighters:** Normal, Big Bruno (hits hard), Baby Johnnys (come in a pack of three).
- **Items:** use one per turn: Mega Punch, Cow Drop, Bomb, Bananas, Chicken Attack, Frog Squad, Kangaroo Boxer. Lose a fighter and you get a revenge crate.
- **Modes:** 2 players or vs Computer, first to 3, 5 or 10.
- **Levels:** Backyard Bounce, Banana Bonanza, Danger Walls, Barnyard Brawl, Bonkers Pinball, Moon Bounce.
