# Trampoline Battle

A ragdoll brawl on trampolines, designed with my kids. Touch the ground and you're out.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any browser. It's one self-contained file and works on desktop, iPad and phones (turn the phone sideways).

## Modes

- **Battle:** both teams drop a fighter from the crane at the same time, then fight. Knock enemies off to score. 10 arenas.
- **Party Levels:** 20 mini-games on trampolines (see below). Rounds to win: 1, 2 or 3.
- **Survival:** no fighting, just stay on. Something new joins every 10 seconds: cows, cannons, sweepers, lava, wind. Solo keeps your best time.
- **Pile-Up:** start with nothing. Every round adds one chaos card and they all stay, so the last rounds are absurd.
- **Endless:** random party level after random party level. Solo gets 3 lives; how many can you clear?

Who's playing: **Solo**, **2 Players** (one keyboard or two sets of on-screen buttons) or **vs Computer**.

## Controls

- **Red:** A / D move, W jump, S slam, F punch, E use item
- **Blue:** ← / → move, ↑ jump, ↓ slam, Enter punch, Shift use item
- Solo or vs Computer: either set of keys controls Red.

## Looks

- **Skins:** Sunday Comics (default), 8-Bit Arcade and Fridge Crayon.
- **Items:** pixel art or inked stickers.

Both switch from the buttons in the header or in the menu, any time.

## Fighters, costumes and weapons

Every fighter gets a random hat, face gear and back piece (capes, wings, jetpacks, tutus…). Weapon crates hand out random weird weapons: fish, frying pan, pool noodle, giant hammer, rubber chicken, plunger, baguette, foam sword, spring glove, giant lollipop, broom, tuba, magic wand, umbrella, leaf blower and pie launcher.
