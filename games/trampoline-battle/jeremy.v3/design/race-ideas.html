100 Race Ideas

Side-view racing with funny cars driven by the same floppy ragdoll fighters. Every round each player gets a random vehicle (bathtub, shopping cart, hot dog, couch, toilet, giant shoe…) and the controls stay simple: gas, brake, hop, honk. Each idea has a little mockup and is ranked by fun + chaos; the 20 we are building first were picked for fun, a spread of rulesets, and what the new engine can do.

FUN 1–10  how hard the kids laugh and yell “again!”
