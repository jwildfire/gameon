How it works

  1. Tell us your game. Talk or type.
  2. Read it back and fix anything that came out wrong.
  3. A grown-up sends it to GameOn on GitHub.
  4. An AI thinks about it, then builds it.
  5. A grown-up checks it, and it shows up in the arcade.

For grown-ups: Send opens GitHub's Game idea form with everything filled in. Read it, take out anything private, and press Submit. Then tell your agent "Evaluate game idea #N".
