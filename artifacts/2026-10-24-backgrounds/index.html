Trippy Backgrounds

Jeremy asked for "some trippy backgrounds. matrix, phsycadelic rainbow. make 20, pick the best 10 and cycle through them", then "make 20 more weird level backgrounds tonight." Here are all forty, running live. Thirty take turns behind the arcade and the New Game page in Weird mode (the Normal | Weird switch by the GitHub button), swapping every 30 to 45 seconds; the Shuffle button in the corner jumps to a random one.

How they were picked: each one was screenshotted behind the arcade at desktop size at 1, 4 and 8 seconds, then judged on wow, on how different they are from each other, on how well the cards and words still read on top, and on speed. A pixel check samples each one 20 times a second for 3 seconds: none flips big areas between bright and dark. The twenty new ones (marked New) were checked at desktop and phone size: no patch of the screen flashes more than three times a second, no more than a few percent of the screen jumps between samples, and phones stay at 30 frames a second. All twenty new ones made the cycle. Code: assets/backgrounds.js.
